## Goal

Bring the admin/ops surface into the UIs, split by posture: the TUI gets the
full set including destructive actions; the WebUI gets a dashboard and
non-destructive ops only, preserving the no-auth network posture.

## Why this plan exists

It locks in the posture rule (destructive + unit management never on the no-auth
surface; non-destructive ops earn a WebUI home) and the one-operations-seam rule
(UIs are thin callers, no duplicated logic). Without it, ops stay CLI-only or,
worse, destructive actions leak onto the LAN-exposed no-auth WebUI.

## Out of scope (do NOT build)

- Auth on the WebUI (deferred bundle; would be its own change if ever).
- Destructive ops on the WebUI/HTTP/MCP (excluded by posture).
- Curation **commit** actions on the WebUI — see open question.

## Open questions to confirm before starting

1. Do curation commits (approve/reject a candidate, synth-from-signal) belong
   in the no-auth WebUI? They are reversible curator writes, not data loss.
   Recommendation: review/inspect in the WebUI, keep the commit in the TUI for
   now; revisit if auth is added.
2. Should `backup` from the WebUI be allowed given it writes a file off-host?
   Recommendation: yes — it is non-destructive to the corpus and read-only over
   the data.
