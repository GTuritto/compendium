## Findings

1. **Qdrant insertion-order non-determinism is real but does not
   trip the per-query gate at `top_k=3`.** The MRR aggregate
   fluctuates between `0.667` and `0.833` across consecutive
   regenerations, but `must_include_slug` is always in the top
   3. The strict baseline-tolerance gate stays informational in
   v0.2 Phase 5; the per-query gate gives reliable signal. This
   matches the design and is documented in
   `docs/operations/retrieval-tuning.md` § "When MRR flaps".

2. **`hnsw_ef` is the wrong knob to tune on a 2-page corpus.**
   v0.2-5.6 demonstrated the loop with a `hnsw_ef` 64 → 96
   change; no metrics moved. Expected: with only a handful of
   vectors per collection, HNSW's candidate-set parameter has
   essentially nothing to expand. The operational doc names
   "bigger / different golden dataset" as the first lever when
   metrics flatline — that's the directionally correct next
   step for the operator who wants more tuning signal.

3. **The synonym filter works end-to-end at the OpenSearch
   layer, independent of the query-time normalizer.**
   v0.2-5.5's raw OpenSearch hit returning `psychological
   safety` at score `1.678` for `body:psych safety` confirms
   the inline synonym filter expands aliases at index time.
   The query-time normalizer is a separate (additive) layer.
